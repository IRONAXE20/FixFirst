# RentBasket Service Knowledge Base (v1)

## How to use this file
- Each issue has an ID, the product, the words customers use, and an ACTION.
- ACTION = TICKET: reply with the Ticket Reply below. Do not suggest any fix.
- ACTION = SELF-FIX: guide the customer through the steps, one at a time. Ask "Did it work?" after the last step. If not fixed, raise a ticket.
- SAFETY FIRST: if the customer mentions smoke, sparks, burning smell, shock, or water near a plug or socket, tell them to switch off at the wall and not touch it, then raise an URGENT ticket.

**Ticket Reply (EN):** "I have raised a ticket for this. Our technician will contact you soon."
**Ticket Reply (HI):** "Maine aapka ticket raise kar diya hai. Hamare technician jaldi aapse contact karenge."

## AC
Brands: Lloyd, Voltas

### AC-01: Not cooling
Customer says: not cooling, cooling kam hai, thanda nahi kar raha, garam hawa
ACTION: TICKET

### AC-02: Compressor issue
Customer says: compressor not starting, outdoor unit not running, bahar wala unit band hai
ACTION: TICKET

### AC-03: Power, plug or socket problem
Customer says: AC not turning on, socket problem, plug problem, light nahi aa rahi, socket jal gaya
ACTION: SAFETY CHECK, then TICKET
1. Ask: "Is the socket burnt or black?"
2. Ask: "Is the wire damaged or is there a short circuit?"
3. If yes to either: "Please switch off the main switch and don't touch the socket." Raise an URGENT ticket.
4. If no to both: raise a ticket.

### AC-04: Filter blocked (weak airflow)
Customer says: hawa kam aa rahi hai, low air, filter jaam, dust
ACTION: SELF-FIX
1. Switch off the AC from the remote and the wall switch.
2. Open the front panel and slide out the filters.
3. Wash them with plain water and let them dry fully.
4. Put the filters back, close the panel, and switch the AC on.
If the problem continues: raise a ticket.

ACTION: TICKET

### FR-04: Water leaking from the front
Customer says: water coming from the front, aage se paani aa raha hai, paani neeche gir raha hai
Cause: the front drain duct is choked.
ACTION: SELF-FIX
1. Switch off the fridge at the wall.
2. Move the food out and keep the fridge door open.
3. Switch on a fan facing the fridge.
4. Leave it like this for 12 hours.
5. Wipe it dry, close the door, and switch the fridge on.
If the problem continues: raise a ticket.

### FR-05: Water leaking from the back
Customer says: water coming from the back, peeche se paani aa raha hai

## Washing Machine
Brand: Whirlpool only

### WM-01: Water not coming in
Customer says: water not filling, paani nahi aa raha, machine mein paani nahi bhar raha
Cause: the inlet valve filter is blocked.
ACTION: SELF-FIX
1. Switch off the machine and close the water tap.
2. Unscrew the inlet pipe from the back of the machine.
3. Pull out the small mesh filter inside the inlet and clean it under running water.
4. Fit the filter and pipe back, open the tap, and start the machine.
If the problem continues: raise a ticket.

### WM-02: Inlet or outlet pipe broken
Customer says: pipe toot gaya, pipe broken, pipe se paani nikal raha hai
ACTION: TICKET

### WM-03: Clothes not washed properly

## Microwave
Brands: Haier, Panasonic, Midea

### MW-01: Sparking and noise
Customer says: sparking, chingari, awaaz aa rahi hai, loud noise
ACTION: SAFETY, then TICKET
1. "Please stop using the microwave and switch it off at the wall."
2. Raise an URGENT ticket.

### MW-02: Not heating (magnetron issue)
Customer says: garam nahi kar raha, not heating, food stays cold
ACTION: TICKET (always)

### MW-03: Capacitor fault (turns on but doesn't work, or no power)
Customer says: chal raha hai par garam nahi, dead, on nahi ho raha
ACTION: TICKET (customer must never open it)

### MW-04: Bad smell
Customer says: smell aa rahi hai, badboo, bad smell
Cause: the inside hasn't been cleaned.
ACTION: SELF-FIX
1. Switch off and unplug the microwave.
2. Wipe the inside walls, door and turntable with a damp cloth.
3. Optional: heat a bowl of water with lemon for 3 minutes, then wipe again.
If it smells like burning, treat it as MW-01.
