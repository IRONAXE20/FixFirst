# RentBasket service and maintenance

- Maintenance and repair are free: manufacturing defects and functional issues — such as an AC not cooling or a washing machine error code — are fully covered; support sends a technician to repair or replace at no cost.
- Service requests are raised through the RentBasket app or the support line.
- Turnaround depends on the issue, location and part availability; if repair isn't feasible, RentBasket may arrange a replacement depending on policy and stock.
- Normal wear and tear is free; major damage from negligence (structural damage, burns, water damage, missing parts) is charged against the deposit.
- Serves Gurgaon and Noida (offices in Sector 52 Gurgaon and Sector 116 Noida).
