# Electric geysers

- Racold instant geysers use a thermostat that cuts power above about 55 °C, with a separate safety cut-out as backup; some ranges self-diagnose and switch off on a malfunction.
- First checks a customer can do safely: the wall switch is on, the indicator light glows, the MCB hasn't tripped, the right (hot) tap or mixer position is used, and the geyser has had enough time to heat (storage geysers typically 15–30 minutes depending on size).
- Resetting a tripped thermal cut-out requires removing the element cover — **this is a technician job**; FixFirst never asks a customer to open the geyser.
- Water leaking near the geyser's switch or wiring, or any tingling from taps, is treated as an electrical hazard.
- Hard water (common in Delhi NCR) shortens element life; scale build-up causes slow heating and noise.
