# Fridge not cooling

Samsung says most cooling problems are fixed by adjusting settings, proper use, checking or cleaning the door seals, or resetting the fridge.

- **Power:** make sure the fridge is firmly plugged into the wall socket (Samsung UK). In Indian homes, also check the voltage stabilizer is on — stabilizers typically delay power by a few minutes.
- **Settings:** about 3 °C for the fridge and −18 °C for the freezer; a knob should not be on 0/off.
- **Door seal:** the door must close fully and the gasket must be clean and undamaged. Paper test: close the door on a sheet of paper; if it slides out easily, the seal isn't tight.
- **Airflow:** leave space behind and beside the fridge and don't block the internal vents with tightly packed food.
- **Direct-cool fridges** (single door) don't defrost automatically. Thick ice on the evaporator blocks airflow and cuts cooling in both compartments; defrost by switching off and letting it melt (Samsung Bangladesh). Never chip ice with sharp objects.
- After being switched off, moved or newly installed, a fridge needs a few hours to cool fully.
- If an error code shows after a power cycle, or none of the above helps, book service.
