# Samsung 4C / 4E and 5C / 5E

## 4C or 4E — water supply problem
The most common cause is a blocked supply hose.
1. Make sure the tap feeding the machine is fully open.
2. Make sure the inlet hose isn't kinked or bent.
3. Check the hose's mesh filter isn't blocked; clean it at least twice a year.

Cleaning the inlet mesh filter: turn off the water, unscrew the hose from the back of the machine, gently pull the mesh filter out of the hose end and rinse it, push it back, screw the hose back on, turn the tap on and check for leaks, then switch the machine off and on.

## 5C or 5E — machine cannot drain
**Safety:** after a hot-water cycle the water inside can be very hot — wait about an hour before starting.
1. Switch off and check the machine is level.
2. Inspect the drain hose — a badly placed drain hose is the most common cause, and service is rarely needed. Route it as the manual shows and make sure it isn't kinked or clogged.
3. Front-load machines: clean the debris filter.
4. Start a cycle; it should drain at the start. If it doesn't, contact customer care for service.

Older models show 4E/5E and newer ones 4C/5C for the same faults. Door errors (dC/dE) and unbalanced load (UE/Ub) are covered on Samsung's information-code pages.
