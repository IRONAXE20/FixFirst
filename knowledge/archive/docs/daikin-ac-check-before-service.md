# AC: check before calling for service

## AC does not run (operation lamp off)
- Has the breaker tripped or a fuse blown?
- Is the plug properly in the socket?
- Is there a power cut?
- Do the remote's batteries need replacing?
- Is the timer set correctly?

## AC does not cool well
- Are temperature and mode set correctly on the remote?
- Are fan speed and airflow direction set properly?
- Are the air filters clean?
- Is furniture or anything else blocking the space below or beside the unit?
- Are windows and doors closed?

## AC stops unexpectedly (lamp flashing)
- Dirty filters: clean them and restart from the remote. If the lamp still flashes, switch off at the breaker and contact service.
- Something blocking the indoor or outdoor unit's air inlet or outlet: remove it and restart.

## Water leaking from the indoor unit
Stop the AC, switch off at the plug or breaker, and contact service. (No customer fix.)

## Remote
- Lost remote: press the ON/OFF button on the indoor unit; mode and fan run on automatic.
- Display faint or remote working poorly: replace both batteries (alkaline, about a year's life).
